Dear Hiring Team,

I'm writing to apply for the Backend Engineer role at Northwind Labs. Over the past two years I've built and shipped production Python services — most recently an order-processing API that handles 40k requests a day and holds 99.9% uptime, which taught me to design for failure rather than around it.

Your posting emphasizes distributed systems and observability. At my last role I introduced structured logging and traced a latency regression to an N+1 query, cutting p95 response time from 800ms to 210ms. I care about the same things you do: systems that stay legible under load.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that same rigor to your team.
